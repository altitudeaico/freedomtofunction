Ministering This Year

The Voices

Prophetess Lydia Chee & Prophet Joseph Lam

Global Breakthrough Ministry · Malaysia

A dynamic ministerial duo ordained by the Fellowship of Ministers and Churches (C.F.N.I., Dallas, Texas), leading the Global Breakthrough Ministry. Together they bring over six decades of combined experience, impacting lives through prophetic insight and deep spiritual healing.

Read More

Prophetess Chee, founder of the School of the Prophets in the North, is celebrated for prophetic worship and teaching that facilitate significant spiritual transformation and healing. Her ministry is marked by powerful deliverances and the movement of the Holy Spirit. Prophet Lam complements this with a strong prophetic and teaching anointing, making complex spiritual concepts accessible and helping dismantle generational curses and emotional wounds.

As a team they conduct workshops, conferences and church camps worldwide — the U.K., U.S., Australia and across Asia — focusing on prophetic ministry and empowering leaders and laypeople alike. Their sessions are often described as transformative, raising a remnant to step boldly into their callings.

Pastor Shade Olatoye

Chief Host & Conference Convener

General Overseer of Calvary Hephzibah Full Gospel Church, leading with dedication and spiritual fervour. Since the passing of her husband in 2020 she has led her congregation with resilience and grace, driven by a vision to bring 'Ekklesia' — the assembly — into every sphere of influence.

Read More

Pastor Shade's leadership is marked by innovative, creative gifting and her ability to foster deep connection within her church and community. A strong advocate for Israel, she aligns her congregation with God's mission through scriptural study, prophetic insight, and practical outreach such as providing food and support to those in need. Her ministry extends beyond the pulpit — authoring books, travelling internationally to spread the gospel, and organising conferences on vital family issues.

A beacon of hope and strength, she is devoted to embodying the love of Jesus Christ, ensuring Calvary Hephzibah is not just a place of worship but a vibrant, active centre of spiritual growth and transformation.

Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a cost to attend?

No. Every gathering is completely free. There is no charge to attend any of the ten stops across the UK tour.

Do I need to register in advance?

Yes, we ask that you register in advance so we can plan for numbers and keep you updated. Registration is quick and takes less than a minute.

What time do the gatherings start?

Times vary by location and day. Please check your specific stop on the tour dates page — times range from morning sessions at 10:00 AM to evening sessions at 7:00–9:00 PM.

Can I attend more than one stop?

Absolutely! Many people travel to attend multiple gatherings across the tour. Each stop is a complete experience with its own flow and ministry.

Who are the speakers?

Prophet Joseph Lam and Prophetess Lydia Chee from Global Breakthrough Ministry (Malaysia) are the primary speakers, alongside various UK-based church leaders and pastors at each stop.

What should I bring?

Bring yourself, an open heart, and a notebook if you like to take notes. Each venue will have seating, so arrive with expectancy for what God wants to speak into your life.

Can children attend?

Yes, children are welcome. Some venues may have children's areas or crèche facilities — check with your local stop for details.

How do I stay updated?

Register on this website and we'll send you updates about your chosen gathering, including final confirmations, prayer updates, and any changes to times or venues.
